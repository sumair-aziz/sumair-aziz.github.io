Research Projects

My research projects combine biomedical signal processing, machine and deep learning, multimodal physiological sensing, and embedded computing to develop intelligent healthcare and neurotechnology systems.

A particular focus of my work is moving beyond offline model development towards practical systems capable of operating with wearable sensors and real-time physiological data.

01

Real-Time Multimodal Pain Assessment

Development of an end-to-end system for objective pain assessment using physiological signals, multimodal deep learning and real-time embedded inference.

Signals

EDA · BVP/PPG

Methods

Signal preprocessing · Deep learning · Multimodal fusion · Transformer-based modelling

Deployment

Wearable sensing · Raspberry Pi · Continuous real-time inference

Earlier Research & Research Leadership

Prior to my current research, I co-founded and led signal and image processing research activities within the Syndicate of Electronic and Embedded Design (SEED) Research Group at UET Taxila. This work included biomedical signal processing, computer-aided diagnosis, biometric authentication and intelligent healthcare applications.
